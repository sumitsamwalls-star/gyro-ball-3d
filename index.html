1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Gyro Ball 3D (Package Name: com.aistudio.gyroball.wkqzjx), developed and published by sam@developer ("we", "us", or "our").

We are committed to protecting the privacy of our players. This document transparently explains what information is collected, how it is used, how it is protected, and your rights regarding your data when using Gyro Ball 3D.

2. Information We Process & Collect

A. On-Device Sensor Telemetry (Gyroscope & Accelerometer)

Strictly On-Device Gyro Ball 3D is a motion-controlled labyrinth game. The application listens to your device's physical gyroscope and accelerometer sensors to simulate real-world gravity and tilt physics.

  • Sensor data is processed in real-time in local memory (RAM) solely during active gameplay.
  • Zero Transmission: Sensor data is never recorded, saved to persistent storage, or sent over the Internet to any remote servers.

B. Optional Account & Cloud Sync Data (Firebase)

Optional Sign-In The app offers an optional cloud sync feature using Google Sign-In via Android Credential Manager and Firebase Firestore.

If you choose to sign in to save your game progress across devices or compete on the global leaderboard, we process:

  • Account Credentials: Basic public profile information provided by Google Authentication (Display Name, Email address, and unique Google User ID / UID).
  • Gameplay Records: Conquered levels, best completion times, earned stars, collected coins, unlocked ball skins/trails, and global leaderboard ranking entries.

Note: If you do not sign in, game progress is stored strictly on your local device.

C. Advertising & Diagnostic Information (Google AdMob)

AdMob Network Gyro Ball 3D displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to keep the game free to play. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic information, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) / Device ID.
  • IP address (used to infer coarse location for regional ad delivery).
  • Device make, model, operating system version, and mobile network carrier.
  • Ad interaction telemetry (impressions, clicks, and load performance).

5. Data Retention & Account / Data Deletion

We respect your right to control your data:

  • Local Data: You can completely erase all locally stored data, high scores, and settings at any time by clearing the application storage in Android Settings (Settings > Apps > Gyro Ball 3D > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the app.
  • Cloud Data Deletion Request: If you have signed in with Google and wish to permanently delete your cloud account, saved records, and leaderboard entries from Firebase Firestore, you can submit a deletion request by emailing sumitsamwalls@gmail.com with your Google account email. All associated Firestore records will be permanently removed within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Gyro Ball 3D is designed for general audiences and casual puzzle game players. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal identification information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information without verified parental consent, we will promptly delete such information from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

7. Security of Your Information

We implement industry-standard security protocols to safeguard data:

  • All network communications between the application and Google / Firebase servers utilize encrypted Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.3).
  • Firestore cloud storage is fortified with strict zero-trust security rules, guaranteeing that only authenticated users can access and update their respective user records.
